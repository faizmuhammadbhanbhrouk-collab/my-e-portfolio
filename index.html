Placement Introduction

My internship placement took place at Spark School, Jinnah Campus, Qasimabad, Hyderabad, from 22nd October 2025 to 18th December 2025. As part of my BS English Language Teaching (ELT) programme at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro, I was assigned to teach English to students of Classes 9 and 10 on Thursdays.

This placement provided me with a valuable opportunity to apply my university learning in a real classroom setting, develop my teaching skills, and understand the responsibilities of an English language teacher.

Placement Details

Throughout my internship, I followed a structured teaching routine that included observing experienced teachers, assisting in classroom activities, and delivering lessons independently. In the beginning, I observed how teachers managed the classroom, introduced new topics, and engaged students in learning. Gradually, I began to take charge of lessons, prepare lesson plans, and design activities that encouraged student participation.

My responsibilities included preparing lesson plans, teaching grammar and reading comprehension, conducting speaking and writing activities, and assessing students' progress. I also maintained a logbook of all sessions, recording the activities, learning outcomes, and reflections for each day.

School Overview

Spark School is a well-organized educational institution that provides a supportive learning environment for its students. The school follows a structured academic programme and encourages both teachers and students to participate actively in the learning process. During my internship, I found the school's environment welcoming and professional.

The teaching staff at Spark School were supportive and helpful. They guided me in lesson planning, classroom management, and interacting with students. The school's focus on discipline, respect, and quality education helped me grow as a teacher and understand the values of professional teaching.

Classroom Context

I taught students of Classes 9 and 10, who were at different levels of English proficiency. Most students were eager to learn but needed support in grammar, vocabulary, and spoken English. To address these needs, I used a variety of teaching methods, including group discussions, role-play activities, worksheets, and interactive exercises.

I tried to create a positive classroom environment where students felt comfortable asking questions and expressing their ideas. I also encouraged them to use English in daily communication, which helped build their confidence and improve their speaking skills.

Key Learning from the Placement

This placement helped me develop several important teaching skills. I learned how to plan lessons effectively, manage classroom time, and handle different learning needs. I also improved my communication skills and gained confidence in speaking in front of a class.

One of the most valuable lessons I learned was the importance of patience and flexibility in teaching. Every class is different, and a teacher must adapt to the needs of the students. This experience taught me to be more reflective and to continuously improve my teaching methods.

Conclusion

My internship at Spark School was a meaningful and rewarding experience. It allowed me to connect theory with practice and develop a deeper understanding of the teaching profession. I am grateful to the school administration, my supervisor, and the students for their support and cooperation throughout my internship.

This experience has strengthened my commitment to becoming a professional English language teacher and has prepared me for the challenges and responsibilities of the profession.

1. Introduction

This report presents a comprehensive account of my internship experience at Spark School, Jinnah Campus, Qasimabad, Hyderabad, from 22 October 2025 to 18 December 2025. As a sixth-semester student of BS English Language Teaching (ELT) at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro, I completed this internship as part of my academic programme to gain practical teaching experience and develop my professional skills.

The internship provided me with an opportunity to apply the theoretical knowledge I gained during my university studies in a real classroom setting. I taught English to students of Classes 9 and 10, focusing on reading, writing, grammar, speaking, and listening skills. This report documents my objectives, activities, reflections, and the lessons I learned during the internship.

2. Objectives of the Internship

  1. Observe experienced teachers to understand classroom management, lesson delivery, and student engagement strategies.
  2. Apply theoretical knowledge of English Language Teaching (ELT) in real classroom settings.
  3. Design and deliver effective lesson plans for Classes 9 and 10.
  4. Communicate effectively with students, teachers, and staff in English.
  5. Develop skills in classroom management and time management.
  6. Create a supportive and interactive learning environment that encourages student participation.
  7. Understand the needs, abilities, and learning styles of students from diverse backgrounds.
  8. Manage classroom behaviour and maintain discipline in a positive and respectful manner.
  9. Reflect on teaching experiences and identify areas for improvement.
  10. Complete the required internship hours and maintain an accurate logbook of activities.
  11. Build confidence as a teacher and develop a professional attitude towards teaching.

3. Description of the Internship Placement

My internship took place at Spark School, Jinnah Campus, Qasimabad, Hyderabad, from 22 October 2025 to 18 December 2025. I taught English to students of Classes 9 and 10 on Thursdays, completing a total of 60 hours over the internship period.

The school provided a supportive and professional learning environment, with well-organized classrooms and helpful teaching staff. I was assigned to teach English language skills, including reading comprehension, grammar, writing, speaking, and listening. My responsibilities included preparing lesson plans, delivering lessons, conducting activities, and assessing student progress.

Throughout the internship, I followed a structured routine that included observing experienced teachers, assisting in classroom activities, and gradually taking charge of lessons independently. I also maintained a detailed logbook of all sessions, recording the activities, learning outcomes, and reflections for each day.

4. Activities Performed

  • Lesson Planning: Preparing detailed lesson plans for each session, including objectives, activities, and assessment methods.
  • Teaching Grammar: Delivering lessons on tenses, active and passive voice, and other grammar topics.
  • Reading Comprehension: Teaching reading strategies and facilitating group discussions on reading passages.
  • Writing Skills: Guiding students through paragraph and essay writing, providing feedback on their written work.
  • Speaking Skills: Organizing role-play activities, dialogue practice, presentations, and public speaking exercises.
  • Listening Skills: Conducting listening exercises and teaching pronunciation rules.
  • Classroom Management: Managing classroom behaviour, maintaining discipline, and creating a positive learning environment.
  • Assessment: Assessing student progress through exercises, assignments, and informal observations.
  • Reflective Practice: Maintaining a reflective journal and logbook to document my teaching experiences and professional growth.

5. Learning Outcomes

  1. Gained practical experience in teaching English to secondary school students.
  2. Improved my lesson planning, classroom management, and assessment skills.
  3. Developed confidence in delivering lessons and interacting with students.
  4. Enhanced my understanding of student needs and learning styles.
  5. Learned to adapt teaching methods to different classroom situations.
  6. Strengthened my communication and interpersonal skills.
  7. Developed a reflective approach to teaching and continuous professional growth.
  8. Gained insight into the daily responsibilities of a professional teacher.

6. Challenges and Solutions

  • Classroom Management: Some students were reluctant to participate in classroom activities. To address this, I used interactive teaching methods, group activities, and positive reinforcement.
  • Different Learning Needs: Students had different levels of English proficiency. I adapted my teaching methods and provided individual support to students who needed extra help.
  • Time Management: Managing classroom time effectively was challenging, especially when activities took longer than expected. I learned to plan lessons with flexible time allocations and prioritize key activities.
  • Adapting to Unexpected Situations: Sometimes, lessons did not go as planned due to unexpected events. I learned to be flexible and adapt my teaching approach to the situation.

7. Reflections on the Internship

My internship at Spark School was a valuable learning experience that helped me grow both personally and professionally. I learned the importance of patience, flexibility, and reflective practice in teaching. I also developed a deeper understanding of the challenges and rewards of the teaching profession.

One of the most important lessons I learned was that teaching is not just about delivering content but about inspiring students to learn and grow. I learned to create a supportive learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and expressing their ideas. I also learned the value of building positive relationships with students and colleagues.

The internship also helped me develop my confidence as a teacher. In the beginning, I was nervous about speaking in front of a class, but with practice and support from my supervisor and colleagues, I gained confidence in my teaching abilities.

8. Supervisor Evaluation Summary

My supervisor evaluated my performance based on criteria including attendance and punctuality, professional conduct, communication skills, teamwork, quality of assigned work, initiative, responsibility, adaptability, and overall performance. The evaluation provided valuable feedback on my strengths and areas for improvement, encouraging me to continue developing my teaching skills.

9. Conclusion

My internship at Spark School was a meaningful and rewarding experience that provided me with practical teaching experience and professional growth. It allowed me to connect theory with practice and develop a deeper understanding of the teaching profession.

I am grateful to the school administration, my supervisor, and the students for their support and cooperation throughout my internship. This experience has strengthened my commitment to becoming a professional English language teacher and has prepared me for the challenges and responsibilities of the profession.

I look forward to applying the skills and knowledge I gained during this internship in my future teaching career and continuing to grow as a reflective and effective English language teacher.
