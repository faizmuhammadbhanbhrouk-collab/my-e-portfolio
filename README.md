# My e-Portfolio

Teaching internship e-portfolio of **Murad Ali** — Bachelor of Science in English
Language Teaching (BS ELT), University of Sindh, Batch 2K24.

## Internship

- **School:** Government Boys Higher Secondary School, Qasimabad, Hyderabad
- **Duration:** 80 hours — 14 September to 30 October 2026 (5 hours a day)
- **Supervisor:** Sir Noor Akhtar Memon
- **Field:** English Language Teaching

## What's inside

A single-page static site (`index.html`, dark theme) with 10 sections:

Home, About, Internship Placements, Internship Objectives, Internship Logbook,
Reflective Journal, Internship Sessions, Supervisor Evaluation, Samples of Work &
Evidence, and Final Internship Report.

It includes the real classroom photos (Samples), the scanned Supervisor's Feedback
form, the handwritten Internship Log, the Final Report, the Internship Completion
Certificate, and the Diploma in English Language (DEL) Certificate of Achievement
from Cambridge Institute of Sure Success (CISS), Qasimabad.

## View it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or enable GitHub Pages on this repository
(Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`).

The site is a multi-file static site: `index.html` plus the `assets/` folder
(classroom photos, scanned documents and PDF files). Keep the `assets/`
folder next to `index.html` for all photos, documents and downloads to work.
